Bond Market

Students issue bonds in their home currency and buy slices from any node. Interledger moves the money; ownership is recorded only once the payment has completed. Coupons and principal are paid under a grant the issuer approves at their bank.

Recurring purchases

or

You approve one grant at your bank, limited per period to about one purchase in your currency plus 5% for rate changes. The first purchase is made when you approve, then one every period. A purchase is skipped, with the reason shown, if the bond is sold out, matured or in default, if you lack the funds, or if the quote is over the grant's limit because a rate moved. Skipped dates still count towards the number of purchases.
